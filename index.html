A FEW DETAILS

Good
to know.

What is The Thought Collection?+

Veleto’s first collection concept: Still Black, Ember Maroon, and Form Slate. Each proposed Ritual Set pairs a matte notebook, tonal dot-grid pages, a coordinating ribbon, and a white-ink companion pen.

Does the paper match the cover?+

That is the design intention. Each color has matching paper and page edges, with white ink for contrast. Physical samples will determine the final shades and writing experience.

Are the drawings already on the pages?+

No. The geometric sketches are examples of use. The proposed pages have only a subtle dot grid, ready for your own notes and ideas.

What comes in a Ritual Set?+

The notebook is proposed at US$19.99. Add an optional pen at US$6.99 to 8.99 or choose a notebook-and-pen bundle at US$24.99 to 27.99. Prices are hypotheses before tax and shipping. Contents, price, and pen design will be confirmed after sampling.

What size is the notebook?+

We are exploring an A5 format with colored paper and a lay-flat binding. Dimensions, page count, paper weight, and materials remain development targets, not confirmed specifications.

When can I order?+

The collection is in development. There is no confirmed launch or delivery date, and orders are not open. This local preview includes a demo waitlist form; it does not subscribe you or take payment.
