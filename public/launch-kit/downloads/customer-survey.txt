VELETO CUSTOMER RESEARCH — CONCEPT, NOT AN ORDER

1. Tell us about the last time you used a notebook.

2. How much do you like this design? Rate 1 to 5 and explain.

3. Which color do you prefer: Black, Maroon, Slate, or none?

4. At $19.99, would you buy the notebook? Why?

5. Would you buy the optional pen at $6.99, $7.99, or $8.99? Answer for each.

6. Would you buy a bundle at $24.99, $26.49, or $27.99? Answer for each.

7. What would stop you from buying?

8. What features do you expect from a premium notebook? Which three matter most?

9. How do you use notebooks today and what did your last one cost?

10. What else would you need to know before buying?
